# Firearm recordings

Source: [The Free Firearm Sound Library](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library)

Recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney. Released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Downloaded 2026-10-18 from `https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Prepared%20SFX%20Library.7z`.

- `revolver-9e32bed6.mp3`: `Prepared SFX Library/Ruger Single Six/S_11P.wav`, segment 0.585–2.285 seconds. Actual single-action revolver shot.
- `gatling-ee9a571c.mp3`: `Prepared SFX Library/PPSh/P_16P.wav`, segment 0.275–1.525 seconds. Actual automatic firearm burst, used as the game's Gatling cue; not a recording of a historical Gatling gun.

Edits: silence trimming, mono downmix, high/low-pass filtering, gain attenuation, peak limiting, fade out, 44.1 kHz / 96 kbps MP3. Two files total approximately 37 KB. Original archive is a build-time source only and is not distributed with the website. The browser loads the two small recordings after the user enables/unlocks sound; gameplay never waits for decoding or download.
