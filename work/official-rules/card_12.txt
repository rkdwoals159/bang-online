
REGOLE CARTA


 Saloon 
Cards with symbols on two lines have two simultaneous 
effects, one for each line.
Here symbols say: “Regain one life point”, and this applies 
to “All the other players”, and on the next line: “[You] regain 
one life point”.
The overall effect is that 
all players in play regain one 
life point
.
You cannot play a 
Saloon
 out of turn when you are losing 
your last life point: the 
Saloon
 is not a 
Beer
!
