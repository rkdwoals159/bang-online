
REGOLE CARTA


 Gatling 
The 
Gatling
 shoots “a BANG!” to “all the other players”, 
regardless of the distance. Even though the 
Gatling
 shoots 
a 
BANG!
 to all the other players, 
it is not considered a 
BANG!
 card
. During your turn you can play any number of 
Gatling
, but only one 
BANG!
 card.
