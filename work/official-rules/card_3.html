REGOLE CARTA

Beer

This card lets you regain one life point – take a bullet from the pile. You cannot gain more life points than your starting amount! The Beer cannot be used to help other players. The Beer can be played in two ways: • as usual, during your turn; • out of turn, but only if you have just received a hit that is lethal (i.e. a hit that takes away your last life point), and not if you are simply hit. Beer has no effect if there are only 2 players left in the game; in other words, if you play a Beer you do not gain any life point.
