
REGOLE CARTA


 El Gringo 
Each time he loses a 
life point due to a card 
played by another player, 
he draws a random card 
from the hands of that player (one 
card for each life point). If that 
player has no more cards, too 
bad!, he does not draw. Note that 
Dynamite 
damages are not caused 
by any player.
